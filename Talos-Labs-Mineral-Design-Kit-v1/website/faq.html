Questions

A few things worth knowing.

What does Talos Labs check?

Your Mac's security settings, using checks aligned with the CIS Apple macOS Benchmark. You get a score and plain-language explanations.

Does Talos Labs upload my report?

No. Checks and reports remain on your Mac, and no account is needed. If you connect ChatGPT or Claude for explanations, the app sends that AI a short summary of the results, never the report, your files or your computer's name.

Does the app run in the background?

Only if you leave the watch on. It re-reads the same settings on a timer you choose. It is not antivirus: it reads settings and start-up lists, never your files, and it cannot see what a program does.

Does it work on Intel Macs?

Yes. All checks and fixes work on Intel and Apple silicon. Explanations by ChatGPT or Claude work on both; the on-device Apple Intelligence option needs an Apple silicon Mac with Apple Intelligence turned on.

Do I need an AI subscription?

No. The check-up and the fixes work with AI off. If you already pay for ChatGPT (Plus or Pro) or Claude (Pro or Max), you connect it once in your browser and Talos Labs uses it for explanations. No API key is needed.

Will it change settings without asking?

No. Every in-app fix has a clear action. Your Mac's own password dialog handles any required administrator authorisation.

Is there a subscription?

A licence costs €24 a year and comes as a key you paste into the app. There is no account. The free version runs the full check-up and fixes the three most valuable things at no cost. What happens at the end of a licence year will be set before launch.

Which macOS versions are supported?

macOS 26 or later, according to the current product brief.
