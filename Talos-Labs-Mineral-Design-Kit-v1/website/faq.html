Questions

A few things worth knowing.

What does Talos Labs check?

Your Mac's security settings, using checks aligned with the CIS Apple macOS Benchmark. You get a score and plain-language explanations.

Does Talos Labs upload my report?

No. Checks and reports remain on your Mac. The app requires no account for a check-up.

Does the app run in the background?

No. With the guided check-up enabled, it checks your Mac when you open it. It does not run scans while closed.

Does it work on Intel Macs?

All core checks and fixes work without Apple Intelligence. The optional on-device AI explanations depend on Apple Intelligence compatibility.

Will it change settings without asking?

No. Every in-app fix has a clear action. Your Mac's own password dialog handles any required administrator authorisation.

Is there a subscription?

No. Talos Labs is a one-time purchase. The exact plan prices and included update period will be set before launch.

Which macOS versions are supported?

macOS 26 or later, according to the current product brief.
