Privacy

Your Mac stays yours.

Talos Labs is designed to check security settings without collecting your private information.

What it checks

Whether your disk is encrypted, your firewall is on, security updates are enabled and screen lock is set up. It can also check network services, background programs, browser extension names and permissions, and developer settings.

What it does not read

Your documents, photos, messages, mail, the contents of your passwords or keys, browsing history, computer name or anything you type into macOS's own password dialog.

What leaves your Mac

Nothing from the check-up. Your report stays on your Mac. If you connect ChatGPT or Claude, Talos Labs sends it a short summary for each explanation: which checks passed or failed, how severe they are, and how you use your Mac. Never a file name, a path, a password or your computer's name. With Apple Intelligence, explanations are generated on your Mac. Purchasing and delivery of a licence key are separate website activities; those processes need their own final privacy terms before launch.

What changes

Nothing without asking. Before an in-app fix, Talos Labs shows what it will change. Administrator authorisation comes from macOS, not from a password field inside Talos Labs.

What it is not

I'm not antivirus. I read your Mac's settings and the list of programs that start on their own, then tell you when something changed. I never read your files. macOS's own virus checks do that part.

  • Not antivirus.
  • No real-time protection.
  • No file scanning.
  • No network traffic inspection.
  • It cannot remove anything.
