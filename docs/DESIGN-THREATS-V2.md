# Talos Labs threat detection v2: more of what the watch and the check-up look at

DESIGN-WATCH put 51 signals behind 21 probes and a rating that code computes and the model may only explain. This document adds the next layer of detection on the same footing: sixteen new signals behind six new probes, four existing signals widened, five check-up rules, two playbook rows, and one screen, "What I watch", that shows the person what the app looks at, what it cannot see, and why. Every new read is a `stat`, a listing, a `defaults read`, an extended attribute name listing (`listxattr(2)`) or a fixed-argument binary; no file contents are opened beyond the two exceptions DESIGN-WATCH already declares, and the one widened here (`codesign` reading an app bundle's signature the way it reads a launch item's program). No new model call, no new notification, no new permission prompt.

This document extends `docs/DESIGN-WATCH.md` (sections 2, 3, 4 and 11), `docs/DESIGN-WATCH-WINDOW.md`, `docs/DESIGN-CHAT.md` and `docs/DESIGN-CHAT-UI.md` (the `open_watch` card), `docs/DESIGN-AI-PRESENCE.md`, `docs/CIS-MAP.md` and `docs/SECURITY-MODEL.md`. Everything in those holds: code collects and judges; the model receives `WatchContext` (ids, booleans, counts, enums, the profile) and never machine text; the model names probe ids and action ids from the closed lists the app put in the request; nothing runs from a model answer; every Mac change is a catalogue entry run after the person's tap, admin ones through macOS's own dialog; no shell, no root; the app is complete with AI off; one primary per guided screen; no notification beyond the watch's act-now; nothing leaves the Mac except the person's own AI calls; British English and the banned-word list in `apps/desktop/src/guided/copy.test.ts`; every model line carries the provider's name and "Not verified". Facts about the tree were checked on 2026-10-17 against the files named; facts about the Mac were checked the same day on macOS 27.0 (build 26A434, arm64, admin account, unsigned dev build, no sudo) with read-only commands only, listed in section 9. `cycle.rs` is being changed by other work; its line numbers may move.

The owner's words, verbatim (2026-10-17): "we need more of the threat detection strategies in place" and "add threat detection strategies and interpretation of daily checks". The interpretation of daily checks is `docs/DESIGN-DAILY.md`; this document is the detection half.

Decisions taken where the brief left room:

- **New signals join the rating rules that exist; no new rating tier.** Every new signal is placed in exactly one of the three existing classes of `apps/desktop/src-tauri/src/watch/signals.rs`: `STAND_ALONE_SIGNALS` (one flip is act now), `GATED_SIGNALS` (asked "Did you just change this?" first) or `PAIR_SIGNALS` (two from different listings within 24 h). The floor and ceiling in `rate.rs::bounds_for` and the hysteresis in `apply_hysteresis` are unchanged. A detection strategy that needs a fourth class was not found.
- **Three new fishy kinds, not seven.** `app` (a new app that skipped the checks), `shell` (a hidden start-up file changed) and `exposure` (the Mac is sharing more than before). Each needs a headline, a why line, a demo signal set, a Details fixture and a harness shot (`FISHY_KINDS` in `packages/core/src/ports/watch-port.ts`, `FISHY.noticed.headline` and `WATCH.why` in `apps/desktop/src/guided/copy.ts`, `SIGNAL_SETS` and `DETAIL_ITEMS` in `apps/desktop/src/adapters/demo-watch.ts`). Everything else folds into the seven kinds that exist: a login hook, a library insert and a masquerading label are `persistence`; authenticated root is `guard`; a PAM file is `admin`; Remote Apple Events is `remote`.
- **`codesign` on an app bundle is the one widened content exception.** DESIGN-WATCH declared "the binary a new launch item names, hashed by `codesign --verify --strict`". The apps probe runs the same `--verify --strict` on a new bundle under `/Applications` or `~/Applications`, then `-dvv` on a bundle that verified, so the closed outcome gains `apple`, `app_store`, `developer_id` and `adhoc` (`-dv` prints no `Authority=` line; verified, section 9). A bundle costs up to two runs and both count against the cap of 10 per cycle (`CODESIGN_CAP`, `signals.rs:404`), shared with launch items, launch items first. The app's own code still reads no bytes. `SECURITY-MODEL.md` "Files the collectors open" and its exec table gain one sentence each (section 7).
- **An OS update is held, not reported.** On this Mac every file under `/etc` the watch would stat (`/etc/zshrc`, `/etc/profile`, `/etc/pam.d/login`, `/etc/paths.d/*`) carries the same mtime, 2026-09-24 08:10:37, the last macOS update. The baseline gains the `sw_vers -buildVersion` string; a cycle in which it changed re-baselines every system-file stat digest silently and the change never becomes an item. The same hold already exists for a Talos fix (`NOISE_HOLD`, `signals.rs`).
- **A masquerading label is one pair signal; an unsigned one is stand-alone.** `sig.launch_label_masquerade` is derived from the persistence listing the probe already reads (label and program path, `apps/desktop/src-tauri/src/collect/macos/persistence.rs:124-147`) and can only rise with `sig.persistence_new` from the same listing (`cycle.rs::derive` computes both from `fresh`), so it stays in the persistence `siblings` group and counts once. The act-now case is a second derived signal, `sig.launch_label_masquerade_unsigned` (row 36): a `com.apple.*` label whose program `probe.persistence_signatures` did not read as `Valid`. It joins `STAND_ALONE_SIGNALS`, as a guard flip does. `pair_group` and the item grouping stay one function.
- **Spotlight is used for one count, outside the cheap sweep.** `mdfind -onlyin ~/Library` for app bundles took 0.73 s here and depends on indexing being on. `probe.library_apps` runs with the full check every 6 h, and at most once an hour when the model names it as a re-read (`min_interval` H1); never in the cheap sweep. When `mdfind` fails it reads `spotlight_off` (section 3).
- **Remote Apple Events is off when macOS does not list it.** `launchctl print-disabled system` shows 12 rows here and no `com.apple.AEServer` row; `systemsetup -getremoteappleevents` needs root. The launchd default is knowable without root: `/System/Library/LaunchDaemons/com.apple.eppc.plist` carries `Label = com.apple.AEServer` and `Disabled = 1` (verified with `defaults read`), so an absent override row means off, as `remote_access::service_loaded` already reads absent labels (`collect/macos/remote_access.rs:42`, test "absent label means off" at line 114). The row joins `sig.remote_access_on` and TL-MAC-033.
- **The "What I watch" screen groups by the five Cyber Essentials controls, with the ATT&CK tactic in a disclosure.** "Firewalls", "Secure settings", "Who can get in", "Malware protection" and "Updates" are words a person can read; "Persistence (TA0003)" is for the son who asks. The screen says the same three things everywhere: watched every few minutes, watched a few times a day, cannot see.
- **No new notification, no new call.** The judge's one call per cycle sees the new signals in `WatchContext.signals` as it sees the old; the daily cap, the gate and the window's rules stand. `probesAvailable` grows by five model-choosable ids.
- **Dropped, with reasons** (each verified on this Mac, section 9): `~/.ssh/known_hosts` growth (8640 B, grows with every developer `ssh`, says nothing alone); preferred Wi-Fi networks (a laptop joins networks; context, not a signal); keychain files (names only, a new file is rare and benign); Find My (`nvram` token unreadable, `com.apple.FindMy` keys undocumented); Fast User Switching (`MultipleSessionEnabled` is 1 here; a setting, not a change worth a headline); the quarantine events database (`stat` shows a download happened; every download does); `/Users/Shared` as a count (23 entries here, the temp-class widening covers programs there); dynamically submitted launchd services (`launchctl print gui/<uid>` lists 472 names; binding each to a file needs 472 more calls); `kern.securelevel` (0 on every Mac); content caching and Bluetooth sharing (CIS level 2 and an unreadable key, `PrefKeyServicesEnabled` absent); Terminal secure keyboard entry; browser homepage and search engine (a Full Disk Access content read and a new exception, open question 5); root certificates (`security` is outside the allowed binaries, open question 6).

## 1. Principles

- **A change against a baseline, in the bad direction, with a second witness.** As DESIGN-WATCH section 1. A lone new item is "something to look at"; act now needs a stand-alone guard flip, a gated flip the person disowns, or two pair signals from different listings within 24 h. No new signal changes this.
- **Strategies are cited, not invented.** Every row in section 2 names the MITRE ATT&CK technique and one published source: the CIS Apple macOS Benchmark (sections as in `docs/CIS-MAP.md`), Apple's Platform Security Guide, Objective-See's KnockKnock categories (the "what experts look at" list), NIST SP 800-61, CISA's #StopRansomware Guide, NCSC Cyber Essentials, ASD Essential Eight. The fishy flow's source lines quote them as DESIGN-WATCH section 4 does.
- **Read-only, no root, no prompt from the background.** Every probe is `stat`, a directory listing, `defaults read`, `listxattr(2)` through `libc` (attribute names only, never values; `libc` is already a dependency, `apps/desktop/src-tauri/Cargo.toml:45`), `mdfind` (outside the sweep) or an `exec::Bin` entry with fixed arguments. Nothing under `~/Desktop`, `~/Documents`, `~/Downloads` or iCloud Drive is ever touched, so no Files and Folders prompt can rise.
- **Machine text stays in the probe.** App names, bundle ids, labels, file names and paths are digested or counted inside the probe function, as today (`probes.rs` header comment). `WatchDetails` is the one document that carries them, fishy Details only.
- **Honest about gaps.** "What I watch" names what needs a signed build, root, Full Disk Access, or is not possible at all, in the person's words, with the same reasons `UNKNOWN_REASON_LABELS` uses (`apps/desktop/src/app/labels.ts:113`).
- **Dev-build truth.** `listxattr`, `codesign --verify --strict` and `-dvv`, `defaults read`, `stat` and `mdfind` all work on the unsigned build; the socket table does not (DESIGN-WATCH section 1), and only `probe.listeners` reads it (`probes.rs:650`). Nothing here widens the gap between the dev build and the Developer ID build.

## 3. Rating rules, extended

`rate.rs::bounds_for` (`apps/desktop/src-tauri/src/watch/rate.rs:56`) and its mirror `packages/core/src/watch/rate.ts` are unchanged in logic. The tables they read change:

- `STAND_ALONE_SIGNALS` (`signals.rs:132`) gains `sig.authenticated_root` and `sig.launch_label_masquerade_unsigned`: 8.
- `GATED_SIGNALS` (`signals.rs:122`) gains `sig.internet_sharing_on`: 7. `GatedKind` gains `exposure`; `FISHY.gate.body.exposure` is "A sharing setting was switched on at {time}." (1.38), true for Internet Sharing and for any later `exposure` gate. `sig.airdrop_everyone` is not gated (row 29): a ten-minute "Everyone" window answered "No, I didn't" would be act now and a notification for a photo.

Not pair, look only: `sig.path_dir_writable_count`, `sig.app_new_unquarantined`, `sig.library_apps_count`, `sig.airdrop_everyone`. An App Store install that adds a login helper raises `sig.app_set`, `sig.app_new_unquarantined` and `sig.persistence_new` within minutes (`bounds_for`, `rate.rs:56-75`, counts pair groups with no severity test, and the signed hold lasts one cycle, `cycle.rs:461`); with row 26 out of the pair set and row 27 needing an unchecked unsigned bundle, that is keep_an_eye, never a notification.

- `siblings` (`signals.rs:251`) gains two groups, `[sig.shell_startup_changed, sig.system_shell_files_count, sig.paths_d_changed, sig.path_dir_writable_count]` and `[sig.app_set, sig.app_new_unquarantined, sig.app_new_unsigned]`, and adds `sig.launch_label_masquerade` and `sig.launch_label_masquerade_unsigned` to the persistence group. `pair_group` stays the first sibling, so a new `/etc/zshenv` (rows 21 and 22 in one tick) is one witness and one item, and `top_items` (`rate.rs:143`) needs no change.
- `severity` (`signals.rs:15`): high for `sig.app_new_unsigned`, `sig.launch_label_masquerade_unsigned`, `sig.login_hook_set`, `sig.authenticated_root`, `sig.pam_auth_changed`, `sig.internet_sharing_on`; medium for `sig.shell_startup_changed`, `sig.system_shell_files_count`, `sig.paths_d_changed`, `sig.path_dir_writable_count`, `sig.app_set`, `sig.app_new_unquarantined`, `sig.library_apps_count`, `sig.airdrop_everyone`, `sig.launch_label_masquerade`, `sig.dyld_insert_set`.

- **Corroboration window.** Stays 24 h (`DAY_MINUTES`, `rate.rs:13`) for every pair. A longer window for the `app` kind was considered (installers finish later) and rejected: one constant, one explanation on the screen.
- **The build hold.** `Baseline` (`baseline.rs:73`) gains `os_build: Option<String>` (`^[0-9A-Za-z]{5,12}$`; "26A434" here, and beta builds carry a lowercase suffix such as `25A5295e`). When `sw_vers -buildVersion` differs from it, the cycle re-baselines `sig.shell_startup_changed`, `sig.paths_d_changed`, `sig.pam_auth_changed`, `sig.pam_sudo_changed`, `sig.hosts_changed` and `sig.sshd_config_changed` without recording a change, then stores the new build.
